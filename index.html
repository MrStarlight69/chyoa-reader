Aero Reader

Your branching stories, beautifully.

Paste a chyoa.com story or chapter link above. The reader shows the chapter and the choices at the end, and can prefetch the next branches so turning the page is instant.

  • Encrypted library — chapters you read are saved in this browser with AES‑256‑GCM, key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 600k iterations).
  • 13 themes — from Frutiger Aero to Webcore. Press T to cycle.
  • Music — add your own audio files or a stream URL; the player keeps going between chapters.

Tip: number keys 1–9 pick a choice, ← goes back.
