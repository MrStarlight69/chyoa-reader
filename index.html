Aero Reader

Your branching stories, beautifully.

Paste a chyoa.com story or chapter link above. The reader shows the chapter and the choices at the end, and can prefetch the next branches so turning the page is instant.

  • Story cipher — chapter text is scrambled in the browser with a substitution cipher. Only someone with the cipher key can read it. Open Cipher to encrypt, decrypt, and share that key.
  • Encrypted library — saved chapters are stored ciphered, then locked again with AES‑256‑GCM. The key comes from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 600k iterations).
  • 13 themes — from Frutiger Aero to Webcore. Press T to cycle.
  • Music — add your own audio files or a stream URL; the player keeps going between chapters.

Tip: number keys 1–9 pick a choice, ← goes back.

        Wiping removes saved ciphers, bookmarks, the music playlist, and the encrypted library. Theme and reading settings stay. Export your ciphers first if you want to keep them.

        Encryption

        Titles, the path, and choice links stay usable. Only the story text is encrypted. With “Encrypt choice labels”, the question and the labels are scrambled too, and the next chapter still loads.

        Juniper, the built-in cipher, folds capitals into lowercase when you decrypt (A and a share an output). Caesar, Reverse, Keyword, and a random shuffle keep every character if each output is used once. Accents and emoji round-trip either way.

        Text size, themes, and the CORS proxy are in the Aa panel. A public proxy can see the chapter URL you request. It does not see the cipher key or your passphrase.
